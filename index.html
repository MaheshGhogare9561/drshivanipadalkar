Before Your Visit

How to Prepare for Your Consultation

A little preparation helps make the most of your appointment.

Bring whatever you can. If you're unsure about something, just mention it during the consultation.

  • Current medicines and creams: bring them, or photos of their labels, including anything bought over the counter.
  • Previous prescriptions and reports: earlier dermatology prescriptions, blood tests or biopsy reports.
  • Photos of flare-ups: if your concern comes and goes, photos taken when it was worse can help.
  • Come without makeup: for facial concerns, a clean face allows a proper examination.
  • Your questions: note down what you'd like to ask so nothing is missed.
Everyday Skin Health

Simple Habits for Healthier Skin & Hair

General guidance that suits most people. Your dermatologist may advise differently based on your skin.

Use Sunscreen Daily

Apply a broad-spectrum sunscreen every day, including indoors near windows, and reapply when outdoors for long periods.

Avoid Self-Medicating

Steroid creams and mixed "fairness" creams bought without a prescription can cause long-term skin damage.

Keep Routines Simple

A gentle cleanser, moisturiser and sunscreen are often enough. Too many active products can irritate skin.

Don't Ignore Changes

Get sudden hair fall, a changing mole, or a rash that won't settle checked early rather than waiting.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I request an appointment?

Fill in the enquiry form below or call the number listed in the contact section. You'll be contacted to confirm a suitable date and time.

What does a dermatologist treat?

A dermatologist diagnoses and manages conditions of the skin, hair and nails. This includes acne, pigmentation, eczema, psoriasis, infections, allergies, hair loss and nail problems, as well as cosmetic skin concerns.

Do I need a referral to see Dr. Shivani?

No referral is needed. You can book a consultation directly.

Can children be seen?

Yes, children's skin and hair concerns can be assessed. A parent or guardian should accompany patients under 18.

Should I stop my current creams before the visit?

Don't stop prescribed medicines on your own. Bring all your current creams and medicines to the consultation so they can be reviewed.

How long will treatment take to show results?

It depends on the condition and the treatment. Many skin and hair conditions improve gradually over weeks to months, and some need ongoing care. You'll be told what to expect for your situation.

Will I need an examination before treatment?

Clinical assessment helps determine the cause of a concern and whether a treatment is appropriate.

Are outcomes the same for everyone?

No. Suitability, response, possible risks and follow-up needs depend on the individual and the proposed treatment.
