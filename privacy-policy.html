2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to personal data we collect through the Website, the enquiry form, phone calls, email and WhatsApp messages about appointments. Medical records created during an in-person consultation are also handled under the medical confidentiality and record-keeping rules that apply to registered medical practitioners in India.

4. Health information

Health information is sensitive. Please share only what is needed to arrange an appointment through the enquiry form. Detailed medical history is best discussed during your consultation.

We treat any health information you share as confidential. We use it only to understand your concern, provide care and keep the medical records required by law.

5. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and schedule, confirm or reschedule appointments
  • To assess your concern, provide consultation and plan follow-up care
  • To send appointment reminders and care instructions by phone, SMS, email or WhatsApp
  • To keep medical and billing records as required by law
  • To keep the Website secure, fix problems and understand how it is used
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from authorities

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We do not use your health information for advertising.

6. Consent and lawful use

We process your personal data with your consent, which you give when you submit the enquiry form or contact us, or for other lawful uses allowed under Section 7 of the DPDP Act. Those include responding to a medical emergency and complying with a law or court order.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us (see section 15). Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already done, or records we are legally required to keep. It may mean we cannot continue to provide some services.

We share personal data only when needed, and only with:

  • Service providers who help us run the Website and practice, such as website hosting ([ADD HOST, e.g. Hostinger]), email, form and appointment tools ([ADD TOOLS]), and messaging services ([ADD, e.g. WhatsApp Business]). They may use your data only to provide their service to us.

10. How we protect it

We use reasonable security measures to protect your personal data, including an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, restricted access to records, and password-protected systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform affected people and the Data Protection Board of India as required under the DPDP Act.

11. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access: ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it
  • Correction and erasure: ask us to correct, complete, update or delete your data, subject to legal record-keeping requirements
  • Withdraw consent: as easily as you gave it
  • Grievance redressal: raise a complaint with our grievance officer and receive a response
  • Nominate: name another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 15. We may need to verify your identity first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

12. Children's information
